<div class="fp-section not-content">
  <p class="fp-kicker">Roadmap</p>
  <div class="fp-roadmap">
    <ol>
      <li>
        <strong>Additional authentication modules</strong>
        <span>SSO, LDAP and OAuth as pluggable identity modules.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>AI chatbot</strong>
        <span>Organiser insight on stats, tickets and feedback — plus guard-railed competitor help.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Custom certificate generation</strong>
        <span>Branded participation and placement certificates from final standings.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Competition replay</strong>
        <span>Scrub back through a finished event and watch the board race again.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Module SDK, importing &amp; marketplace</strong>
        <span>A documented SDK, installable modules, and a marketplace with verified publishers.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Programming challenges</strong>
        <span>Programming and secure-coding formats as first-class competition types.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Cheat detection engine</strong>
        <span>Flag-sharing and anomaly signals surfaced to judges — evidence, not auto-bans.</span>
      </li>
    </ol>
    <div class="fp-roadmap-note">
      Sequence, not dates — shaped in the open
      on <a href="https://github.com/tbcsec/flagpost/discussions">GitHub Discussions</a>.
      The fuller story lives at <a href="https://flagpost.io/#roadmap">flagpost.io</a>.
    </div>
